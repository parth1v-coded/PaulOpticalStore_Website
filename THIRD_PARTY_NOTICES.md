## Photographs
All product and site photos are from Wikimedia Commons under open licences (CC0, public domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA). Each photo's author, licence and source link is listed in [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md). If you reuse a photo, follow *its* licence (for example CC BY and CC BY-SA require credit; CC BY-SA also requires sharing adaptations under the same terms).

## Paul Optical logo
The "PO" monogram and the Paul Optical name/branding are not covered by any open licence. They are the shop's own artwork, shown here as part of the portfolio demo. Please don't reuse them as your own branding.
